> **TL;DR:** Over 80% of local beauty searches end up at a business in the Google Local Map Pack. By setting the correct beauty categories and regularly collecting photo-rich reviews, your salon can secure these premium leads.

## The Power of Local Pack for Beauty Salons

When clients search for hair, nail, or spa treatments, they want convenience and high-quality results. Most users search for terms like *"nail salon near me"* or *"best hair spa near me"* directly on Google Maps. Because of this, optimizing your [salon Google Maps ranking](/services/seo) is the most effective way to drive walk-ins and book appointments online.

Ranking in the Local Pack requires optimizing several key relevance signals: proximity, categories, listing details, and review velocity. Salons that build a complete, search-engine-friendly profile can secure the top three spots, capturing local demand without paying for expensive ads.

---

## Steps to Dominate Google Maps Listings

### Step 1: Claim and Verify Your Salon Listing
Verify ownership of your Google Business Profile and ensure your name, address, and phone number are up to date.

### Step 2: Choose Specific Beauty Service Categories
Instead of using a generic "Beauty Salon" category, add specific primary and secondary categories like *"Hair Salon"*, *"Nail Salon"*, or *"Facial Spa"*.

### Step 3: Encourage Photo-Rich Client Reviews
Ask clients to upload photos of their hair or nail results along with their Google reviews. Listings with user-generated photos receive significantly more views and higher search authority.
